Terms Of Service

> WHAT DO I DO:

I specialize in game editing and also do motion design. While I have my own editing style, I'm highly adaptable and committed to tailoring my work to your specific vision. From fast-paced gaming content to any other video style, I guarantee your project will be edited with precision and purpose.

> DEADLINE:

The deadline is chosen by the customer, however, a minimum of 48 hours is required for editing.

> EDITING PROGRAMS:

I use Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects.

> SEND FILES:

The client must send all videos, audios, and files via Google Drive. The completed video is delivered via Google Drive.

> UNFORESEEN EVENTS:

If there are any unforeseen circumstances on my part that prevent the video from being delivered, the client will be notified in advance. Unexpected problems can come from either side, including the client themselves, for example: something wrong with the video, choppy or failing audio, delayed video, errors during recording, etc. Things like that are usually only noticed during the process, which can, rarely, delay the video editing progress.

> PAYMENTS:

There are two ways to make the payment: Sending half of the agreed amount before the video is delivered and the other half afterward, or: Sending the total amount in advance - Payment in full after the video has been made and delivered is not accepted. Payment is made via PayPal or Inter Bank.
